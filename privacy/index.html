Magnus Harness

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This policy explains how the Magnus Harness desktop app for macOS handles Google user data. Contact jaribwetshi64@gmail.com with any question about it.

Google user data the app accesses

Magnus Harness accesses Google data only for the Google accounts you connect in the app's Settings, and only after you allow access on Google's sign-in screen. It uses these Google APIs:

  • Google Calendar API: your calendars, events, free time and invitations.
  • Gmail API: your messages, threads, drafts and labels.
  • Google Drive API: your files. Google Docs, Sheets and Slides are read as text.
  • Google Photos Picker API: only the photos you pick in Google's photo picker. The app cannot read your whole photo library. It can also read photos that Magnus Harness itself uploaded.

How the app uses Google user data

  • The agent in a chat uses your Google data to answer your requests and to do the tasks you ask for, such as finding events, reading mail or opening a file. When several accounts are connected, it can read across all of them and names the account in its answer.
  • Changes are made only through actions in the chat: creating, updating, moving or deleting events, answering invitations, creating drafts, sending, labelling, archiving or marking mail as read, creating, uploading or editing files, and uploading photos or creating albums. The chat's permission setting decides whether the app asks you before each change.

How Google user data is stored

  • The app on your Mac calls Google directly. Magnus Harness has no server, so your Google data does not pass through or get stored on any server run for Magnus Harness.
  • The sign-in tokens Google gives the app are stored on your Mac in the app's database, with a copy in the macOS Keychain. They are never written to logs or shown in chat tool results.
  • Google data the agent reads becomes part of that chat, which the app keeps on your Mac with your other chats.

How Google user data is shared

  • Magnus Harness chats run on the AI model account you connect in the app, such as your Claude account with Anthropic. When the agent reads Google data in a chat, that data is sent to that AI model service as part of the chat, under your own account, so the model can answer you. That service's own terms and privacy policy apply to it.
  • The developer of Magnus Harness does not receive your Google data, because the app has no server.
  • Apart from that AI model service, your Google data leaves the app only through actions the agent takes in the chat, such as sending an email or sharing a file. The chat's permission setting decides whether the app asks you before each such action.

Limited Use

Magnus Harness's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove access

  • In the app, open Settings, Google, and choose Disconnect on the account. The app revokes its access at Google and deletes the account's tokens from your Mac.
  • At any time you can also remove Magnus Harness's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
